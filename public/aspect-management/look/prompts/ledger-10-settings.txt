A Hebrew, right-to-left web app called Aspect Management: the internal system a small Israeli AI company (Aspect) uses to run its own operations — developers' daily hour reports, projects, clients and price quotes. The owner, Itzik, and a few developers use it every day. It already has six screens in this exact look; this is one more screen of the same product.

Imagine the system as a beautifully kept work ledger: warm ivory paper, charcoal ink, ruled lines, margin marks and occasional stamped colour. Avoid glossy UI, gradients and floating tiles; information should feel written, checked and filed, yet completely digital and fast. Use dark forest green, oxblood and muted ochre as sparse functional accents. Hebrew typography has an editorial, bookish character, with bold handwritten-like annotations used only for actions. The mood is familiar, grounded and accountable - like opening one reliable company notebook where nothing gets lost.

This screen is "הגדרות": who is on the list of the system. It holds: the title "הגדרות" with the line "מי נמצא ברשימה", one primary action "+ אדם חדש", and one line per person with the name in bold, the email, and a small tinted tag for the role ("בעלים" or "מפתח", or "לא פעיל" in grey). Example people: איציק (בעלים), יוסי לוי (מפתח), דנה שמש (מפתח), רון אברהם (לא פעיל). A quiet note: "הגישה עצמה למערכת נפתחת בנפרד". All text in Hebrew.
